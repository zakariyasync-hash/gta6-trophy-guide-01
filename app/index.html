      The road to the platinum — 3 stages, about 200 hours

      Built from the official descriptions and PowerPyx's roadmap (difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies). Keep cheats off — PowerPyx notes cheats disable trophies and saving. Because there is no mission replay, keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter.

      After the credits you return to free-roam and the 100% checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Nothing on that checklist is missable.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed, according to PowerPyx — and even that one is still being confirmed as required. Everything else is repeatable in free-roam after the story.

      Not missable — but easy to worry about

      • Story mission trophies (12). They pop when the mission ends. No mission replay exists, but you cannot fail a story trophy by playing the story.
      • Side-quest givers who go quiet. Kenton is unavailable for a stretch after Chapter 1 and returns in Chapters 2–3; all trophy-related quest givers are back in free-roam after the story.
      • Optional missions that expire. Some disappear after a while, but there are far more than the 100 the checklist wants, so none of them locks a trophy.
      • Collectibles in story buildings. A few magazines sit in buildings that later close, but the same items spawn elsewhere and no trophy needs all of them.

      Source: PowerPyx — trophy guide and roadmap, including its note on missable content.
